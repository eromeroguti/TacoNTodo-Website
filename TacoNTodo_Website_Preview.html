Our Story

TacoNTodo started with a simple passion: to bring authentic Mexican flavors and the warmth of community gathering to every event across the Coachella Valley. Founded in Cathedral City, we believe that great food brings people together. Every taco we serve is made with fresh, quality ingredients and respect for traditional recipes — because we know that when you're celebrating, your catering should be exceptional.

We're proud to serve Cathedral City, Palm Springs, Rancho Mirage, Palm Desert, Indio, La Quinta, and beyond. Whether it's festival season, wedding season, or any moment worth celebrating, TacoNTodo is here to make it memorable.

Our Services

From intimate family gatherings to large-scale festivals, TacoNTodo offers flexible catering solutions tailored to your needs. Every service includes our commitment to authentic flavors and professional execution.

Taco Cart Catering

Our signature mobile taco cart brings the full taqueria experience to your location. Fresh tortillas, premium meats, house-made salsas, and all the fixings — prepared right in front of your guests. A visual and culinary centerpiece for any event.

Party & Event Catering

Birthday parties, graduation celebrations, family reunions — any gathering is better with tacos. We handle everything from setup to cleanup, so you can focus on what matters: enjoying time with your guests.

Wedding Catering

Make your reception unforgettable with a custom taco bar. We work with you to create a menu that reflects your taste, style, and vision for the day. Professional service, beautiful presentation, and authentic flavors.

Corporate Events

Team lunches, office parties, client appreciation events. Professional service that makes you look good and keeps your team engaged. We bring the same quality to corporate environments as we do to celebrations.

Festival & Large Events

Experienced with high-volume service for festivals, community events, and large gatherings across the Coachella Valley. We've served crowds at Coachella Festival, Stagecoach, and major community celebrations.

Custom Taco Bar Setup

Build your own experience. Choose your proteins, toppings, salsas, and sides for a fully customized spread. Perfect for guests who want variety and the option to create their ideal taco.
