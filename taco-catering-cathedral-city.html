Catering That Started
Right Here in Cat City.

Cathedral City isn't just where we cater — it's home. TacoNTodo's kitchen is based right here on Estio Road, and we've spent years feeding the celebrations that make this city special, from backyard quinceañeras and birthday parties off Date Palm Drive to corporate lunches near the Cathedral City Auto Center and the Civic Center.

When you book us for a Cathedral City event, you're getting a hometown caterer who shows up early, cooks everything fresh on-site, and treats your guests like neighbors. Whether it's a graduation, a company party, or a wedding near Mary Pickford Theatre, we bring the full mobile setup and an authentic Mexican menu built around your day.

Cathedral City Venues

Catering Our Own Backyard

Cathedral City is home. Our kitchen is on Estio Road, which means shorter travel, easier logistics, and the flexibility to handle last-minute details other caterers can't. We regularly serve backyard celebrations across the Date Palm Drive corridor, community events near the Cathedral City Civic Center and its amphitheater, and team gatherings and tournaments at Big League Dreams Sports Park.

Family events are the heart of what we do here. Quinceañeras, baptisms, graduations, birthdays, and anniversary parties make up much of our Cathedral City calendar, and those events run on a different rhythm than corporate work — bigger extended families, longer evenings, and a lot more grandchildren circling the taco station. We staff accordingly.

Being local also means we know the venues, the neighborhoods, and how to get a mobile kitchen into a tight residential driveway without drama. If you're hosting within city limits, we can usually accommodate a shorter booking window than we'd promise elsewhere in the valley.
