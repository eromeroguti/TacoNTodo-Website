PrivacyPolicy

How we handle the information you share with TacoNTodo.

Last updated: July 6, 2026

TacoNTodo (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the website tacontodops.com. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have when you visit our site or request a catering quote. By using our website or submitting our quote form, you agree to the practices described below.

Information We Collect

We only collect information you choose to give us, plus basic anonymous usage data:

  • Quote form details. When you request a quote, we collect your name, email address, phone number, event date, event type, estimated guest count, and any message you send us.
  • Analytics data. We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use our site — pages viewed, approximate location (city/region), device and browser type, and referring source. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your catering inquiry and prepare a custom quote.
  • To contact you about your event by phone or email.
  • To improve our website, menu, and service based on aggregate usage trends.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing.

Cookies & Tracking

Our site uses cookies and similar technologies through Google Analytics to measure site traffic and performance. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time; the site will continue to work, though some usage measurement will stop. We do not use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking.

Third-Party Services

We rely on a few trusted providers to run our website and respond to inquiries. Each has its own privacy practices:

  • Google Analytics — website usage measurement.
  • Vercel — website hosting and delivery.
  • Resend — delivers your quote-form submission to us by email.

Data Retention

We keep quote inquiries only as long as needed to respond to and service your event, and for reasonable business record-keeping. You may ask us to delete your information at any time using the contact details below.

Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information you’ve shared with us. To make a request, contact us at the phone number or email below.
